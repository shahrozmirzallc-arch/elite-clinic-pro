Privacy Policy

Your Privacy and Health Information Security

Last Updated: January 2024

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Elite Clinic Pro, protecting your privacy and safeguarding your health information is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share your personal and health information in compliance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and other applicable privacy laws.

Information We Collect

Health Information

We collect and maintain Protected Health Information (PHI) necessary to provide quality healthcare services, including:

  • Medical history and current health conditions
  • Diagnostic test results and imaging
  • Treatment plans and medications
  • Physician notes and consultation records
  • Billing and insurance information
  • Immunization records

Personal Information

We also collect personal information to facilitate your care:

  • Name, date of birth, and contact information
  • Social Security number (for billing and identification)
  • Insurance information and policy details
  • Emergency contact information
  • Employment information (when relevant to care)

Website and Portal Information

  • Login credentials and account information
  • IP address and browser information
  • Usage data and preferences
  • Communication records (emails, messages)

How We Use Your Information

For Treatment

We use your health information to:

  • Provide, coordinate, and manage your healthcare
  • Consult with other healthcare providers involved in your care
  • Refer you to specialists or other healthcare facilities
  • Maintain continuity of care across our provider network

For Payment

We use your information to:

  • Bill you or your insurance company for services
  • Verify your insurance coverage and benefits
  • Process payment and manage accounts
  • Collect outstanding balances

For Healthcare Operations

We use your information to:

  • Improve quality of care and patient safety
  • Train healthcare professionals
  • Conduct business planning and management
  • Ensure compliance with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Conduct quality assessment and improvement activities

For Communication

We may contact you for:

  • Appointment reminders and confirmations
  • Treatment follow-ups and care coordination
  • Health education and wellness information
  • Service announcements and facility updates

How We Protect Your Information

Security Measures

We implement comprehensive security safeguards:

  • Physical Security: Restricted access to facilities, secure file storage, visitor controls
  • Technical Security: Encrypted data transmission, secure servers, firewall protection, regular security updates
  • Administrative Security: Employee training, confidentiality agreements, access controls, security policies
  • Regular Audits: Ongoing monitoring and assessment of security practices

Employee Training

All staff members receive comprehensive HIPAA training and are required to:

  • Maintain patient confidentiality at all times
  • Access only information necessary for their job duties
  • Use secure communication methods
  • Report any potential privacy breaches immediately

When We Share Your Information

With Your Authorization

We will share your health information with third parties only when you provide written authorization, except as described below.

Without Your Authorization (As Permitted by Law)

We may share your information without authorization in these situations:

  • Treatment Coordination: With other healthcare providers involved in your care
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or subpoena
  • Public Health: For disease reporting, vaccine tracking, or public health investigations
  • Safety: To prevent serious threat to health or safety
  • Law Enforcement: For criminal investigations or in response to lawful requests
  • Workers' Compensation: For workplace injury claims
  • Business Associates: With vendors who provide services (under strict confidentiality agreements)

Your Privacy Rights

You have the following rights regarding your health information:

Right to Access

You may request to inspect and obtain copies of your medical records. Some limitations may apply.

Right to Amendment

You may request corrections to your medical records if you believe information is incorrect or incomplete.

Right to Accounting of Disclosures

You may request a list of certain disclosures we've made of your health information.

Right to Request Restrictions

You may request limitations on how we use or share your information, though we are not required to agree to all requests.

Right to Confidential Communications

You may request that we communicate with you in a specific way or at a specific location.

Right to a Paper Copy

You may request a paper copy of this Privacy Policy at any time.

Right to Notification of Breach

You have the right to be notified if your health information is breached.

Website Privacy

Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies to enhance your experience. We collect:

  • Usage data to improve website functionality
  • Analytics to understand visitor behavior
  • Session information for logged-in users

Patient Portal Security

Our patient portal uses:

  • Encrypted connections (SSL/TLS)
  • Multi-factor authentication options
  • Automatic session timeouts
  • Secure password requirements

Children's Privacy

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Parents or legal guardians must provide consent for treatment of minors and access to their health information through our portal.

Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy. Any changes will be posted on our website and made available at our clinics. The revised policy applies to all information we maintain.
