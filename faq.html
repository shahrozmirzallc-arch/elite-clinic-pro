Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

Appointments & Scheduling

How do I schedule an appointment?

You can schedule an appointment in several ways:

  • Online through our booking system (available 24/7)
  • Call us at (555) 123-4567 during business hours
  • Visit any of our clinic locations
  • Through our patient portal mobile app

How far in advance should I book?

For routine appointments, we recommend scheduling 1-2 weeks in advance. For specialists, booking 2-4 weeks ahead is advisable. However, we do accommodate same-day and next-day appointments for urgent matters.

Can I request a specific doctor?

Yes! When booking your appointment, you can select your preferred physician. If your first choice isn't available, our system will show alternative time slots or suggest other qualified physicians in the same specialty.

What is your cancellation policy?

We request at least 24 hours notice for cancellations to avoid a cancellation fee. You can cancel or reschedule through our patient portal, by phone, or email. Same-day cancellations may incur a $50 fee.

Insurance & Payment

What insurance plans do you accept?

We accept most major insurance plans including Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Medicare, and Medicaid. Visit our Insurance page for a complete list or call to verify your specific plan.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we offer flexible payment plans for qualifying patients. Our billing department works with you to create a payment schedule that fits your budget. Interest-free options are available for balances over $500.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, checks, all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), debit cards, HSA/FSA cards, and online payments through our patient portal.

Will I receive a bill?

Yes, after your insurance processes the claim, you'll receive a statement for any remaining balance (copay, deductible, or non-covered services). Statements are sent via mail or email based on your preference.

First Visit & New Patients

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring:

  • Photo ID (driver's license, passport, etc.)
  • Insurance card(s)
  • List of current medications
  • Relevant medical records or test results
  • List of questions or concerns
  • Payment method for copay or deductible

How early should I arrive?

Please arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment time. This allows time to complete paperwork, verify insurance, and update your medical information. New patients should arrive 20-30 minutes early.

Can I complete forms before my visit?

Absolutely! You can access and complete all new patient forms through our patient portal. This saves time at check-in and helps us prepare for your visit.

Services & Specialties

Do you offer telemedicine visits?

Yes! We offer virtual visits for many types of appointments including follow-ups, consultations, medication management, and minor concerns. Video visits are available through our secure patient portal.

Do you have urgent care services?

Yes, we provide same-day urgent care appointments for non-life-threatening conditions. For emergencies, please call 911 or visit your nearest emergency room.

Can I get lab work done on-site?

Yes, we have a full-service laboratory at our main location and most of our satellite clinics. Most results are available within 24-48 hours and sent directly to your physician.

Do you offer preventive care services?

Absolutely! We provide comprehensive preventive care including annual physicals, wellness exams, immunizations, health screenings, and preventive counseling. Many preventive services are covered 100% by insurance.

Medical Records & Privacy

How can I access my medical records?

You can access your medical records 24/7 through our patient portal. You can also request copies by completing a medical records release form, available at any clinic location or through our website.

Is my health information secure?

Yes, we take privacy seriously. We are fully HIPAA compliant and use industry-leading security measures including encrypted communications, secure servers, and strict access controls. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I send my records to another provider?

Yes, you can authorize us to send your records to any healthcare provider. Complete a release form specifying which records and where to send them. There is typically no charge for electronic transfers.

Prescriptions & Pharmacy

How do I request a prescription refill?

You can request refills through:

  • Our patient portal (fastest method)
  • Calling our pharmacy line
  • Having your pharmacy contact us directly

Please allow 24-48 hours for refill requests.

Can you send prescriptions to any pharmacy?

Yes, we can electronically send prescriptions to most pharmacies nationwide. Simply provide your preferred pharmacy information when requesting medications.

Location & Hours

What are your hours of operation?

Our hours vary by location:

  • Main Clinic: Monday-Friday 7 AM - 7 PM, Saturday 8 AM - 4 PM
  • Most satellite clinics: Monday-Friday 8 AM - 6 PM
  • Urgent Care: 7 days a week, 8 AM - 8 PM

Check our Locations page for specific hours at each clinic.

Is parking available?

Yes, all our locations offer free parking for patients and visitors. Handicapped-accessible parking is available at all entrances.
